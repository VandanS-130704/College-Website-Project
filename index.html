A Legacy of Excellence

Located in the heart of Nagpur, Maharashtra, Shri Ramdeobaba College of Engineering and Management (RCOEM) has been a beacon of academic brilliance and innovation since its inception in 1984. Built on a sprawling 20-acre eco-friendly campus, RCOEM has consistently nurtured holistic development, bridging the gap between theoretical knowledge and real-world industrial demands.

As a premier autonomous institute affiliated with Rashtrasant Tukadoji Maharaj Nagpur University, we take immense pride in our state-of-the-art infrastructure, highly experienced faculty, and rigorous curriculum. From securing top-tier placements in global tech giants to fostering a culture of research and entrepreneurship, RCOEM is committed to transforming today's students into tomorrow's leaders.
